THE BOARD OF REGENTS

Under Republic Act No. 8292 (Higher Education Modernization Act of 1997), the governing body of a chartered state university is vested in the Board of Regents. As the governing body of the Polytechnic University of the Philippines, the Board exercises general powers of administration and the specific powers and duties provided under the law.

The Board of Regents promulgates and implements policies in accordance with the declared State policies on education, the Philippine Constitution, and the policies, standards, and thrusts of the Commission on Higher Education (CHED). It also exercises the powers and performs the duties necessary to carry out the purposes and functions of the University, as provided under Republic Act No. 8292 and other applicable laws.

THE BOARD OF REGENTS

The Polytechnic University of the Philippines is governed by the Board of Regents, the University’s governing body. Its composition is established pursuant to Republic Act No. 8292, otherwise known as the Higher Education Modernization Act of 1997, and the laws governing the Polytechnic University of the Philippines. The Board brings together representatives from the government, the University, the faculty, the student body, the alumni, and the private sector to provide policy direction and exercise governance over the University in accordance with its mandate and applicable laws.

Office of the University/Board Secretary

The Office of the University/Board Secretary serves as the official secretariat of the Polytechnic University of the Philippines Board of Regents. Pursuant to Republic Act No. 8292, the Board appoints a Secretary who serves as Secretary of both the Board and the University. The Board Secretary is responsible for keeping the records and proceedings of the Board and for communicating notices of Board meetings to its members. Through these functions, the Office supports the proper documentation and orderly conduct of the Board’s official proceedings.
